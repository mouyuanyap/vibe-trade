- **Market Position:** Apple maintains a dominant position in premium smartphones (~75% share of $800+ segment), tablets (~55%), and smartwatches (~35%). The ecosystem moat (iOS, iCloud, App Store, Apple Pay) creates switching costs that are among the highest in any consumer industry.

- **Regulatory Risk:** EU Digital Markets Act continues to pressure App Store economics. Canada's new digital bill (May 2026) saw Apple and Meta jointly opposing. DOJ antitrust case (filed 2024) remains an overhang but resolution is unlikely within this 30-day window.

**Geopolitical Risk:** The Intel foundry deal partially mitigates Taiwan strait risk for Apple's chip supply. However, US-China tensions remain elevated — any escalation in tariff policy would impact Apple both directly (China manufacturing, ~15–20% of revenue) and indirectly (consumer spending, input costs).
