### Competitive Landscape & Moat Assessment

| Dimension | AMD Position | Trend |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Data Center CPU | EPYC ~35% unit share, gaining vs Intel | Widening |
| AI GPU | MI350X: ~5-7% revenue share vs NVDA ~80% | Narrowing gap |

| Software ecosystem | ROCm improving but still behind CUDA | Narrowing slowly |

**Key competitive assessment**:
- **vs NVIDIA**: AMD's MI350X matches B200 on FP8 compute (4,600 TFLOPS) and exceeds it on memory (288GB vs 192GB HBM3E). However, NVIDIA's CUDA software maturity delivers 50-55% Model FLOPS Utilization vs AMD's ~45%. Software is the battleground. AMD's open-source ROCm strategy is gaining traction with hyperscalers who want a second source.
- **vs Intel**: Intel remains behind in process technology and data center CPU. Intel Foundry struggles create an opening for AMD to continue gaining share. However, Intel's 18A node and Panther Lake could narrow the gap in 2027-28.
- **Custom ASIC threat**: Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Microsoft Maia are emerging but primarily cannibalize low-to-mid-range GPU deployments, not the high-end training clusters where AMD and NVIDIA compete.

**Moat rating**: **Widening, but asymmetric**. CPU moat is strong and growing. GPU moat is real but still a distant #2 to NVIDIA.
