**Parallel 1 — 2017-2018 Tech Rally:** Following the 2015-16 earnings recession, tech stocks rallied strongly on the AI/data center buildout and tax-cut tailwinds. **Parallel 2 — 2000 Dot-Com Bubble (extreme case):** Internet infrastructure buildout and "new economy" narrative drove tech multiples to unsustainable levels. Key difference: Google in 2026 generates $400B+ in actual revenue with 36% operating margins — this is real earnings, not dot-com promises. The risk is not that Google is a bubble, but that current multiples price in AI growth that may accrue more slowly than the market expects.
