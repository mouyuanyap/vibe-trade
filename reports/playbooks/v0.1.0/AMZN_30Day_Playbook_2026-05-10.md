### Competitive Landscape & Moat Assessment

Amazon's moat is anchored by five structural advantages:

1. **AWS infrastructure leadership** — AWS commands ~31% cloud market share with $37.6B in Q1 revenue. The moat is reinforced by: (a) custom silicon (Graviton/Trainium/Nitro at $20B+ run rate) providing cost advantage and NVIDIA independence, (b) the broadest service portfolio (200+ services), (c) enterprise lock-in via data egress costs and ecosystem integration. AWS operating margin of 37.7% demonstrates pricing power despite competition.

2. **Logistics & fulfillment density** — Amazon's $200B+ logistics infrastructure is a barrier to entry that no competitor can replicate. Same-day/next-day delivery for 1B+ items in Q1 2026 reduces cost-per-unit while increasing customer switching costs. Prime's ~230M global members create a recurring revenue flywheel.

3. **Advertising profit engine** — Amazon's ad business ($17.24B in Q1, TTM >$70B) is growing at 24% with software-like incremental margins. Sponsored Products, Prime Video ads, and Twitch inventory provide diversified ad surfaces. This is the structural driver of retail margin expansion — every ad dollar flows through at 70%+ incremental margin.

4. **AI full-stack position** — AWS AI services at $15B+ run rate, custom Trainium/Inferentia chips, Bedrock managed AI platform, and Amazon Q (AI coding assistant) position Amazon across the AI stack. The $200B capex plan, while compressing near-term FCF, is building the infrastructure for the next decade of AI workloads.

5. **Diversification across uncorrelated profit pools** — AWS (37.7% margin), advertising (70%+ incremental margin), third-party seller services, and Prime subscriptions create a portfolio where retail cyclicality is buffered by high-margin recurring revenue streams.

**Threats to the moat:**
- **$200B capex ROI uncertainty**: FY2026 capex of ~$200B (Q1: $44.2B) is an unprecedented infrastructure bet. TTM FCF collapsed to $1.2B. If AI demand doesn't materialize at projected levels, ROIC will be severely impaired.
- **Azure AI momentum**: Microsoft's Azure grew 40% in Q1 with 12 points from AI services. The OpenAI partnership gives Azure a unique AI workload magnet that AWS is competing against.
- **Retail margin ceiling**: North America retail at 9.0% margin may be approaching a ceiling. Wage inflation, unionization pressure, and antitrust scrutiny (FTC lawsuit) could compress margins.
- **Antitrust risk**: The FTC antitrust lawsuit (filed 2023) is ongoing. While remedies are uncertain, any forced separation of AWS from retail or restrictions on seller practices would damage the flywheel.
- **Consumer cyclicality**: A recession scenario would pressure both retail revenue growth and ad spending simultaneously.
