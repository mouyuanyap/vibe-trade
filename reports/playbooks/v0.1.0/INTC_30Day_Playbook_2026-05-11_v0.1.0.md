### Cash Flow Quality Assessment

INTC is in a **heavy investment / expansion phase**: CFO positive (+$9.70B FY2025), CFI deeply negative (-$14.82B), CFF positive (+$11.59B). This is the classic "Expansion" quadrant (CFO +, CFI -, CFF +). The company is generating operating cash but borrowing/investing heavily for the foundry buildout.

**Key concern:** FCF was -$4.95B in FY2025 and -$15.66B in FY2024. This negative FCF trajectory is unlikely to reverse before 2028-2029 when foundry investments begin generating returns. The balance sheet is solid enough to fund this, but the cash burn is substantial.

INTC's GAAP ROE is negative (-2.9% per yfinance) due to heavy investment amortization and restructuring charges. A meaningful DuPont decomposition requires using non-GAAP metrics:

| Component | INTC (Non-GAAP approx) | AMD | NVDA |

**Drive:** INTC's ROE is suppressed by low margins (foundry investment) and low asset turnover ($211B asset base vs $54B revenue). This is a capital-intensive turnaround — ROE expansion depends on revenue growth filling the massive asset base.

Customer concentration: Historically diversified across PC OEMs (Dell, HP, Lenovo) and cloud providers. No single customer >15%. The Apple deal would add significant customer concentration risk to the foundry segment.
